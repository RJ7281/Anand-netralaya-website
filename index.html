ABOUT THE HOSPITAL

Trusted eye care in Virar since 2008

Anand Netralaya Hospital is one of the leading and most trusted eye care hospitals, having started its journey in 2008. Its path so far has built experience and expertise across a range of clinical and diagnostic procedures, including phacoemulsification with foldable IOL implantation.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment to visit Anand Netralaya?

While walk-ins are always welcome during consulting hours, patients with prior appointments are given priority to minimize waiting time.

Where is Anand Netralaya located?

Anand Netralaya Eye Care Hospital is conveniently located in Virar West, Maharashtra, offering accessible eye care facilities for all local and outstation patients.

What are your phone numbers?

You can reach our hospital reception directly at +91 99230 25465 for consultations, booking inquiries, or emergency guidance.

Which eye conditions do you treat?

We provide specialized care across seven specialty clinics including Cataract (Phacoemulsification), LASIK, Retina & Cornea, Glaucoma, Pediatric & Squint evaluation, and Dry/Lazy Eye management.

How long does a typical visit take?

A comprehensive consultation including vision testing and pupil dilation usually takes around 1.5 to 2 hours.

Can I drive home after my pupil is dilated?

No. Dilating drops cause temporary blurriness and light sensitivity for 2 to 3 hours. We recommend bringing an accompanying person or arranging public transportation.

What documents should I bring for my consultation?

Please bring your previous medical prescriptions, past eye test reports, current spectacles, and a valid photo ID.

Is cashless insurance facility or claim assistance available?

Yes, our desk assists with major medical insurance policies and reimbursement documentation for surgical procedures.
