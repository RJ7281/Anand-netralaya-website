Cataract Clinic

Today, due to remarkable advancements in IOL technology, a patient can achieve excellent vision quality from a lens implant without needing glasses at all. A lens implant, or intraocular lens (IOL), is an implanted synthetic lens that replaces the eye's natural lens. Depending on the type selected, an IOL can also correct the eye's existing refractive error (nearsightedness or farsightedness), and a patient who has received an IOL can still have their vision further improved with laser vision correction.

  • Foldable intraocular lens (IOL) implantation
  • IOL options that also correct nearsightedness or farsightedness
  • Vision can be further refined later with laser correction
  • Surgery performed in a dedicated, air-locked operation theatre

Retina & Cornea Clinic

The retina is the innermost nerve layer lining the back of the eye — many ophthalmologists compare it to the film of a camera. The retina processes the images projected onto it, and the optic nerve then transmits these to the brain. The retina is susceptible to many types of disease. At Anand Netralaya Hospital we are also dedicated to treating problems involving the cornea and other anterior structures of the eye such as the eyelids, conjunctiva, sclera, anterior chamber, iris and lens.

  • Diagnosis and treatment of retinal disease
  • Corneal care and management
  • Treatment of the eyelids, conjunctiva and sclera
  • Anterior chamber, iris and lens evaluation

Glaucoma Clinic

Glaucoma is one of the major causes of blindness. It is a group of conditions in which high pressure inside the eye (intraocular pressure) damages the optic nerve. Glaucoma usually affects both eyes. It is very hard to stop glaucoma completely, but years of research show that treating the disease early helps preserve vision. The primary effect of any glaucoma treatment is lowering IOP, which has been proven to be an effective way to prevent or slow vision loss.

  • Intraocular pressure (IOP) measurement and monitoring
  • Optic nerve assessment
  • Early treatment to help preserve vision
  • Long-term follow-up for both eyes

Pediatric Clinic & Squint Evaluation

We provide treatment for an extensive range of paediatric eye problems, including sight-threatening conditions such as paediatric glaucoma, cataract and congenital opacities of the cornea. We also provide the latest available Diagnostic Squint Service. Squint is a misalignment of the two eyes, so that both eyes are not looking in the same direction. This misalignment may be constant, present throughout the day, or it may appear only sometimes, with the eyes straight the rest of the time.

  • Paediatric glaucoma and cataract treatment
  • Congenital opacities of the cornea
  • Latest available Diagnostic Squint Service
  • Assessment of both constant and intermittent squint

Phacoemulsification

Our patients can avail hygienic and patient-friendly phacoemulsification with a foldable lens. Phacoemulsification refers to modern cataract surgery in which the eye's internal lens is emulsified with an ultrasonic handpiece and aspirated from the eye. Aspirated fluids are replaced with irrigation of balanced salt solution, thus maintaining the anterior chamber as well as cooling the handpiece.

  • Foldable lens implantation
  • Ultrasonic emulsification of the natural lens
  • Balanced salt solution maintains the anterior chamber
  • Hygienic, patient-friendly surgical environment

Dry & Lazy Eye Evaluation

Dry eye is a condition resulting from inadequate or ineffective wetting and lubrication of the eye. It primarily occurs due to a reduction in the quality or quantity of tears and the tear film. Amblyopia (lazy eye) is poor vision in an eye that did not develop normally during childhood. It commonly affects one eye but may involve both. It is generally caused by lack of use of one eye, when the brain "favours" one eye over the other.

  • Evaluation of tear quality and quantity
  • Tear film assessment
  • Amblyopia (lazy eye) diagnosis
  • Management for one or both affected eyes
