PATIENT GUIDELINES

What to expect on your visit

Please read these guidelines before you arrive. Following them keeps your time with us short and your consultation focused.

Before you come

  • Dilating drops blur your vision for two to three hours — please arrange for someone to drive you home.
  • Bring any previous prescriptions, spectacles and eye records with you.
  • Walk-ins are welcome, but appointment patients are seen first.
1

Appointments get priority

Patients with an appointment will be given priority over walk-in consultations.

2

Register at reception

Register yourself with all demographic details at the front reception desk upon arrival.

3

Enroll your name

Enroll your name in the daily consultation queue list at the counter.

4

Preliminary examination

A preliminary vision examination will be performed by the optometrist in the refraction room.

5

Dilating drops

Refraction will be followed by instillation of eye drops for pupil dilation required for detailed retinal examination.

6

Doctor consultation

Detailed clinical slit-lamp assessment, diagnostic evaluation and treatment guidance with Dr. Anand Raut.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical visit take?

A comprehensive consultation including dilation usually takes around 1.5 to 2 hours.

Can I drive after pupil dilation?

No. Dilating drops blur near vision and cause glare for 2 to 3 hours. Please arrange for someone to drive you home or use public transport.

Are walk-ins accepted?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome during consulting hours, though appointment holders are given priority.

What should I bring on my visit?

Please bring your previous medical prescriptions, current spectacles, and any past eye medical records.

Ready to book your eye check-up?

No prior appointment required and walk-ins are always welcome — but booking ahead means you are seen first.
