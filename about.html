Anand Netralaya Hospital is one of the leading and most trusted eye care hospitals, having started its journey in 2008. Its path so far has built experience and expertise across a range of clinical and diagnostic procedures, including phacoemulsification with foldable IOL implantation.

The hospital is fully equipped with advanced digital imaging, laser and surgical technologies, and consistently imports peer-approved technologies from across the world. It is known for excellence in ophthalmic services with personalised care, and is committed to pioneering the technological revolution in eye care.

The center comprises comfortable, homely recovery rooms and a separate operation theatre complex with HEPA filter equipped with laminar airflow systems and air-locked lobbies. Every subspeciality is attended by a qualified and experienced ophthalmic surgeon, supported by diagnostic setups from the respective subspeciality clinic.
